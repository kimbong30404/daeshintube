Our Story

Built on the
Standards of K-Beauty

Daeshin was founded in 2007 with a straightforward purpose: to manufacture PE tubes that meet the exacting standards of Korea's cosmetics industry. Over nineteen years, that purpose hasn't changed — only our scale and capability have.

Our clients include some of the most demanding OEM manufacturers and fastest-growing K-beauty brands in Korea. Supplying them has sharpened every aspect of our process — from raw material selection to final QC — to a level that transfers directly to any brand we work with.

In 2024, we relocated to a purpose-built facility in Paju. The new factory was designed from the ground up for cleaner production, better climate control, and the capacity to meet growing demand without compromising quality.
